# C0LONY roadmap

C0LONY is a daily ant-colony game on Robinhood Chain, paid in $WIF. Season 1 is what we built for the World is Flat hackathon. This page covers what ships in Season 1 and where the game goes after it.

## Season 1 (hackathon build)

- Found a colony with WIF, feed the queen with WIF at least every 72 hours, and grow by digging chambers, building leafcutter trails and aphid farms, and assigning ants.
- Sealed raids for leaves and sugar, settled with drand evmnet randomness verified onchain. Defenders get a guard window, shields cost WIF, and a raided player gets one free **Avenge** raid back at the raider within 24 hours.
- WIF from founding, feeding, speed-ups, shields and revives splits 70% to the season pot, 20% burned and 10% to the treasury. Sponsors can add to the pot. Colonies that finish in the top 25%, still alive and fed at least twice, share the pot.
- Embedded wallets with no transaction pop-ups, sponsored gas for new players, X profiles, a live in-app raid feed, welcome-back summaries, push alerts and an installable phone app.
- A Voter's Pass discount for wallets that voted with WIF in World is Fair.
- Each colony is an NFT minted at founding. **During a season it cannot be transferred**, so nobody can buy their way into the pot.

## After the hackathon

### 1. Season NFTs (first priority)

- When a season ends, every colony that is still alive **unlocks as a tradable Season NFT**. It records the season, final rank, score, raid record, founding day and a pixel snapshot of the colony as it ended.
- Colonies that fell during the season stay locked as a keepsake.
- **Burn for a perk**: burning a Season NFT gives its owner a perk in the next season. Perks are cosmetic or a small head start (for example a queen's crown, a rare chamber skin, or a discount on founding), never anything that buys rank or pot share.
- **Marketplace**: list and buy Season NFTs in WIF inside the app. A royalty on each sale goes to the treasury, which tops up the next season's pot. Trading old seasons feeds the new one.

### 2. Deeper colonies

- **Fungus garden**: a new building that turns leaves into sugar, giving leaf-heavy colonies a way to grow.
- **Journal finds**: the cosmetic items you dig up become things you can display on your profile, and later wear in the colony art.
- **Weekly neighbourhood standings**: small groups of nearby colonies compete each week for bragging rights and a share of sponsor drops.

### 3. More seasons and more ways in

- Seasons on a regular schedule, each with a theme and a sponsor slot.
- Sponsor tools: a page for projects to fund a season pot and get their name on the season.
- More sign-in options and a Farcaster mini app, so the game can live where crypto players already are.

Dates depend on how Season 1 goes. We will update this page after it ends.
